Data Deletion Instructions — TijaraAI

Last updated: July 19, 2026

If you would like your account and all associated data (orders, products, messages, and Facebook/Instagram connection details) deleted from TijaraAI, you can request deletion in either of the following ways:

Option 1 — Email request

Send an email to habibomar425@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and the email address associated with your account. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Option 2 — Revoke app access via Facebook

You can also remove TijaraAI's access to your Facebook account at any time from your Facebook settings: Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → find TijaraAI → Remove. This revokes our access to your Page and Instagram data immediately.

Once a deletion request is received, all merchant account data, connected Page/Instagram identifiers, order history, and stored messages associated with that account will be permanently deleted from our database.
