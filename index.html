Privacy Policy — TijaraAI

Last updated: July 19, 2026

TijaraAI ("we", "our", "the platform") provides tools for merchants to manage orders, products, and customer messages received through Facebook and Instagram. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how merchants and their customers can contact us with questions.

1. Information We Collect

  • Merchant account data: name, email, and Facebook user ID, collected when a merchant logs in with Facebook.
  • Facebook Page and Instagram data: Page ID, Page name, linked Instagram account ID and username, and a Page access token, collected when a merchant connects their Page.
  • Customer messages: the text content of messages sent by customers to a connected merchant's Facebook Page or Instagram account, along with the customer's platform-assigned sender ID (PSID).
  • Order data: customer name, phone number, delivery address, and product details, either entered manually by the merchant or extracted automatically from customer messages using AI.

2. How We Use This Information

  • To authenticate merchants and give them access to their own dashboard.
  • To receive and automatically reply to customer messages on a merchant's behalf, using AI to generate responses and extract order details.
  • To let merchants manage orders, products, inventory, and delivery tracking.
  • To provide merchants with analytics such as revenue, order counts, and rejection rates.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell customer or merchant data. Message content is sent to a third-party AI provider (Groq) solely to generate a reply and extract order details; it is not used by that provider to train models on our behalf. Facebook Page access tokens are used only to send replies and manage message subscriptions via Meta's official APIs, and are never shared with third parties.

4. Data Retention

Merchant accounts, orders, products, and message history are retained for as long as the merchant's account remains active. Merchants may request deletion of their account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see Section 6).

5. Data Security

Access tokens and account credentials are stored securely and are only accessible to authenticated merchant sessions via their own account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access.

6. Contact Us

For questions about this policy, or to request data deletion, contact us at: contact@tijaraai.example.com

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
